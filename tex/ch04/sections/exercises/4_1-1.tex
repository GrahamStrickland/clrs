% Exercise 4.1-1
When every element of $A$ is negative, \textsc{Find-Maximum-Subarray} returns a
single-element subarray $(i, i, A[i])$, where $A[i]$ is the largest, i.e. the
least negative, element of $A$.

To see this, note that appending any further element to a subarray of a wholly
negative array strictly decreases its sum. Every subarray of two or more
elements therefore has a sum smaller than that of its largest element taken
alone, so the maximum is always attained by one of the one-element base cases of
the recursion.
